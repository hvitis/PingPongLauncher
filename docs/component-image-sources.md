# Component image sources and reference records

This document records the source photographs, vector illustrations, visual evidence, rights statements, and limitation notes for each component in the bill of materials.

## Purpose and scope

Photographs and SVG illustrations in this build guide serve visual identification only:

- **Listing photo (`listing`)**: Photographed from candidate supplier listings (Botland) to help identify commercial items. Printed pocket fits, batch tolerances, and electrical ratings must still be verified against the physical parts.
- **Reference (`reference`)**: Representative product or hardware family photographs from manufacturer or distributor listings. Used to illustrate general physical appearance; dimensions, grades, lengths, or electrical characteristics may differ from the source specification.
- **Material (`material`)**: Depicts raw material stock (such as a 3D-printing filament spool) rather than a finished or custom-fabricated component (such as a safety guard).

WARNING: These illustrations and photographs depict physical appearance only. They are not dimensioned engineering drawings, CAD models, or certified proof of fit or compatibility. Copyright in product photographs remains with the respective source photographers and retailers.
